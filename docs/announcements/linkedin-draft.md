We’ve released **OpenTDCR**: hardware designs and simple teleoperation code developed for our tendon-driven continuum robot experiments at the University of Toronto’s Continuum Robotics Laboratory.

It includes modular 3D-printed components, CAD, Python teleoperation, and a configurable tendon-calibration workflow that can use Lyra or your own measurement sensor. We’re sharing tools that helped our research in the hope they’ll be useful to others.

Explore the design: https://continuumroboticslab.github.io/OpenTDCR/
Code and hardware: https://github.com/ContinuumRoboticsLab/OpenTDCR

Feedback and contributions are welcome!
