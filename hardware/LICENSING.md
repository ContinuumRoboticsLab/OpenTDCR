# Hardware licensing status

The software MIT notice credits the Continuum Robotics Laboratory and Governing Council of the University of Toronto, and preserves the original 2024 Chengnan Shentu notice for inherited code. This software attribution does not change the licensing of CAD or supplier components. No new hardware license has been selected in this release candidate.

Before public redistribution, the maintainers must identify which CAD objects they own, select the license for their mechanical designs, and confirm rights for any third-party objects included in the STEP assembly. A name or product number in a CAD file is evidence of provenance to investigate; it is not permission to redistribute.

The supplied STEP assembly includes product names associated with ROBOTIS XL-430 servos, U2D2, U2D2 Power Hub Board, JST-style connectors, and fasteners. Their original source URLs and redistribution terms were not supplied. Preserve required notices or replace/remove supplier geometry if its terms do not permit the intended distribution. The custom connector PCB also needs its own schematic/layout source and licensing record if it is to be reproduced.

The release checklist should record:

- The copyright holder and selected license for each custom mechanical source file.
- The source and license for each supplier CAD model retained in `assembly.step` and derived visual assets.
- Permission to redistribute the assembly photos.
- Whether the final downloadable hardware archive is a complete build release or reference-only CAD.

`asset-manifest.json` records the exact bytes reviewed. Update it when an asset changes. Until these items are resolved, describe the hardware as a release candidate with licensing pending rather than a fully licensed open-hardware release.
